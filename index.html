FAQ

Why do YouTube comment GIFs download as WebP?

YouTube serves comment GIFs as animated WebP because it is smaller than GIF. Many apps don't accept WebP, so this tool converts it back to a real GIF.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion happens in your browser. Dropped files never leave your device. Pasted YouTube URLs are fetched through the wsrv.nl image proxy because YouTube blocks direct cross-site downloads.

Which formats can I convert?

Input: GIF, animated WebP, APNG, PNG, JPEG, MP4, WebM and MOV. Output: GIF, animated WebP, APNG, MP4 (H.264) and WebM (VP9).

Which browsers work?

Recent Chrome, Edge, Opera and Firefox, on phones, tablets and desktops. Safari doesn't support the image decoder yet.

The GIF is too big. What can I do?

GIF is an old format. Pick a smaller size, or convert to WebP or MP4, which are often 5 to 10 times smaller.
